Tools for a better school experience

From reporting an issue to understanding recurring problems, SchoolFix helps make school improvement more organized and transparent.

📣

Easy issue reporting

Students can submit school concerns with a category, location, description, and priority.

🔎

Report tracking

Students can revisit their reports and check the latest status as issues move forward.

🛡️

Administration dashboard

Authorized administrators can review reports, assign teams, and update issue status.

📈

School analytics

View report categories, resolution progress, and a simple overview of school concerns.

🧠

Pattern insights

Explore prototype priority suggestions and recurring issue patterns to guide attention.

☁️

Firebase cloud support

Configured accounts can use Firebase Authentication and Firestore to share report data.

Simple by design

From concern to action in three steps

A clear workflow helps everyone understand what needs attention and what happens next.

01

Spot it and report it

A student describes the issue and adds its location so the right people can understand it.

02

Review and assign

Administration checks reports, sets priorities, and assigns the appropriate team.

03

Track the improvement

Statuses are updated as the school works toward resolving and verifying the issue.
